Babies cry for reasons that are hard to read at 3 a.m. CrAI Baby listens
to a few seconds of crying and suggests what's most likely behind it —
hunger, discomfort, tiredness and more — using a machine learning model
that runs entirely on your phone. Recordings are never uploaded, and the
clip is discarded as soon as it has been analyzed.
