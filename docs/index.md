# CrAI Baby

CrAI Baby helps parents and caregivers understand why their baby is
crying. Record a short audio clip and the app classifies the likely
cause — hunger, discomfort, tiredness, and more — using an on-device
machine learning model. No internet connection is required, and
recordings are never uploaded anywhere: everything happens locally on
your phone. You can also correct a prediction, and the app learns from
that feedback to get better over time on your device.
