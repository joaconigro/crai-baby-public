## What the app does

CrAI Baby records a short audio clip through your device's microphone
and uses an on-device machine learning model to suggest a likely reason
a baby might be crying — hunger, discomfort, tiredness, and similar
categories. It may also show a short, general caregiving tip alongside
that suggestion.

## Accuracy

The model produces a statistical guess, not a fact. Babies cry for
reasons that overlap, change, and often have nothing to do with the
sound of the cry itself. Background noise, microphone quality, clip
length, and the individual baby all affect the result.

Predictions will be wrong sometimes — sometimes confidently wrong. The
app is offered as a starting point for your own attention, and we make
no promise about how accurate any particular result is.
