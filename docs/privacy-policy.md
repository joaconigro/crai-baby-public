# Privacy Policy for CrAI Baby

**Effective date:** September 17, 2026

CrAI Baby ("the app") helps parents and caregivers understand why a baby
might be crying by listening to a short audio clip and classifying it
on-device. This policy explains what happens to that audio and to any
other data the app touches.

## Audio recording

When you tap to analyze a cry, the app records a short clip (a few
seconds) using your device's microphone.

- **The audio is processed entirely on your device.** It is analyzed by
  an on-device machine learning model to classify the likely cause of
  crying.
- **The audio is never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to any server.**
  The app does not require an internet connection to analyze a cry, and
  it does not send audio to us, to the developer, or to any third
  party.
- **The audio clip is not saved.** Once a clip has been analyzed, it is
  discarded from memory. The app does not keep a library or history of
  recordings.

## Data stored on your device

If you correct a prediction (e.g., telling the app the actual reason
for a cry), the app saves that correction — the predicted label, your
corrected label, and a timestamp — locally on your device only. This is
used to fine-tune future predictions for your device and is never
uploaded anywhere. It contains no audio and no information that
identifies you personally.

This data stays on your device and is removed automatically if you
uninstall the app.

## What we do collect

If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to
[Sentry](https://sentry.io), a crash-reporting service, to help us find
and fix bugs. This report contains technical details about the crash
(such as device type, OS version, and the error itself) but never
audio, corrections, or any information that identifies you personally.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect usage tracking or advertising identifiers.
- We don't require account creation, so we don't collect names, emails,
  or other personal information.
- We don't share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties beyond
  the anonymous crash reports described above.

## Children's privacy

CrAI Baby is intended to be used by parents and caregivers, not by
children. The app does not knowingly collect any information from
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this
page with a new effective date.
